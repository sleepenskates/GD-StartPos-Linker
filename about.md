# Level Linker

Links an online level with one of your created levels so you can switch
between them with a single click. Handy when you're re-creating a level in
the editor and want to compare your progress with the original.

## Usage

1. Open any online level's info page (or open one of your created levels in
   the editor menu).
2. Click **Link** - a link will be "pending".
3. Open the other level (your created one, or the online one) and click
   **Link** again. The two levels are now linked.
4. Click **Switch** on either level to jump directly to the other one.

Clicking **Unlink** on a linked level removes the link.
